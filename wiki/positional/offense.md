# Offense

When we have it, or just won it. The four who have the ball or are creating the next pocket.

Taking space, rotations, pivot as the high reference, support under the ball, finishing at near and far post. Cue is pass-and-move. Standing after a pass is an offense error.

Flying goalie, when relevant, is a note here and on [[goalie]] (`goalie`). Not a fourth unit.
