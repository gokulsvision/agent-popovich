# Starting position

Canonical for this slug.

## Rule

Height by third of the pitch.

- Ball in their half: high. You are a sweeper behind the line, not a spectator on the six.
- Ball in the middle third: 6-yard box to the penalty spot. Not the goal line.
- Shot possible: 3–6 yards off the line. On the ball-to-centre-goal line.

Rec keepers lose races because they start on the goal line. Train the start position first. Then train the race.

## Cue

Where is the ball. Where is our last man. You should be high enough to win a ball played in behind, low enough to set if the shot is on.
