# Distribution

Simple first. Be a pass option. Do not play like City.

## Rule

Short to the free player. Long if pressed and no free player. Throw if the target is open and closer than a kick. Do not dribble into traffic because a pro compilation did.

Amateur translation of Ederson / Alisson: show for the pass, take it under no pressure, give it back or find the free player. That is the whole trick.

## Cue

First look is the free player. If there is not one, go long and let the rest of us compete.
