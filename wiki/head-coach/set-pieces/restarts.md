# Restarts

Goal kicks, free kicks, throw-ins. Simple first.

## Rule

Goal kick: short if we have a free player. Long if we do not. Do not invent a build-out against a press this team has not trained.

Free kick in our half: one taker, everyone else behind the ball until it is played.

Throw-in: take it. Do not wait for a picture that will not arrive.

## Cue

First option is the free player. If there is not one, go long and compete.
