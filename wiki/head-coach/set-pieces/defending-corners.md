# Defending corners

One routine. Hybrid. Not pure zonal, not pure man.

**Format:** 11v11.

## Routine

- Goalie owns the six-yard box and calls
- One on each post
- Near-post runner picked up
- Penalty-spot runner picked up
- First man out for the clearance
- Rest of the box: nearest man, then drop

Amateur hybrid. We justify it with a zonal-vs-hybrid pro case when one exists. Until then, this is the job list.

## Cue

You have a job before the ball is kicked. If you do not know it, you are the gap.
