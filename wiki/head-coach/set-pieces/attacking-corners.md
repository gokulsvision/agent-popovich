# Attacking corners

One routine. Names and jobs, not a blocker circus.

**Format:** 11v11. Futsal restarts are not this page.

## Routine

Inswinger. Near post occupied. Penalty spot occupied. One on the edge for the clearance. Three behind the ball.

Jobs, not names, until TEAM.md names them:

- Taker
- Near post
- Penalty spot
- Edge
- Rest defence (three)

## Cue

Arrive at the job. Do not ball-watch from the six. If you are rest defence, you do not join the six-yard box.

## Do not copy

Arsenal's full decoy package. Rec teams will not time six coordinated runs.
