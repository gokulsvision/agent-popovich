# Transitions

The five seconds after a win or a loss.

## Rule

Win: take space that is free. See [[taking-space]] (`taking-space`).
Loss: rest defence first, then recover. See [[rest-defence]] (`rest-defence`).

Do not train these as two different sports. Same five seconds. Opposite jobs.

## Cue

The moment the ball changes feet, the picture changes. First body movement in that window is the whole note.
