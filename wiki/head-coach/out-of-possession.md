# Out of possession

When we do not have it, or just lost it.

## Rule

One press trigger. Otherwise drop. The trigger is a bad touch or a pass we can reach. If the trigger is not there, we drop as a block. Chasing as five individuals is how rec teams get run through.

## Cue

Ball lost. Count. If we cannot win it in the next two seconds, drop. See [[press-or-drop]] (`press-or-drop`).
