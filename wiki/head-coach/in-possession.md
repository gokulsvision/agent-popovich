# In possession

When we have it, or just won it.

## Rule

Keep it simple. Support under the ball. One player high as a reference. Do not all run toward the ball. The free player is created by the ones who leave, not by the ones who crowd.

## Cue

First look forward. If it is not on, keep it. Standing after a pass is still an error. See [[taking-space]] (`taking-space`).
