# Taking space

Canonical. Other paths stub here.

**Format:** principle. Distances live on the match page (`11v11` or `futsal`).

## Rule

When the ball is won and the next pocket is free, go there. Pass, then move. Width first, then height. Do not take space that is occupied. Do not take space that leaves nobody behind the ball.

Standing still after a pass is an offense error.

## Cue

Win it. First thought forward. If the lane in front of you is empty, occupy it. If it is not, hold and show for the next pass.

## When not

The back line is already stretched and a loss becomes a 3v1. Rest defence first. See [[rest-defence]] (`rest-defence`).
