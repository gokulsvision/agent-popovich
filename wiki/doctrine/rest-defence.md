# Rest defence

Canonical. Other paths stub here.

**Format:** principle. Numbers live on the match page.

## Rule

When we have the ball in their half, enough players stay behind it that a loss is not a break the other way. Rec default: three behind the ball after an attacking corner. Two is a gamble. One is a concession.

## Cue

Before the cross or the shot, count bodies behind the ball. If the count is short, the highest player is not the problem. The rest of us are.

## When not

A dying second and a must-score. Even then, the goalie does not join a corner unless the match page says flying goalie.
