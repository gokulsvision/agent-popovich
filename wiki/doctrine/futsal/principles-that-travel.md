# Futsal principles that travel

Not a futsal syllabus. Only what survives on an 11v11 rec pitch.

## Rule

Pass and move. Rotations to create a free player. Near post and far post occupied on a break. First thought forward. Goalie as a pass option, not as a playmaker.

A futsal sweep is not a 40-yard race. Do not quote court geometry on a pitch. Match pages carry `format: 11v11` or `format: futsal`.

Flying keeper stays a footnote until this team actually pulls the keeper.

## Cue

After you pass, you are not done. The next body into the vacated space is the point.
