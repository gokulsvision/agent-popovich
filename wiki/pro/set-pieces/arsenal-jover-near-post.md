## Situation

Premier League corner. Inswinger. Attackers start deep of the far post, then time a run as the ball is struck. Near post is flooded. Penalty spot is attacked. Second ball is hunted.

## Decision

Jobs, not chaos. Someone occupies near post. Someone occupies the penalty spot. The rest of the circus is optional.

## Why it worked

Zonal defenders are static. A timed run from behind the zone arrives in a gap they cannot cover. The near-post flick or the penalty-spot header is the point.

## Amateur translation

One taker. One near post. One penalty spot. One on the edge. Three behind the ball. That is the whole routine. Rec teams will not time six decoy runs. See [[attacking-corners]] (`attacking-corners`).
