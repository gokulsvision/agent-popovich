# Neuer vs Algeria, World Cup 2014 — sweeper actions

Problem it teaches: come-off-the-line
Level: elite. Use as a picture of the decision, not as a copy of the starting height.
Format: 11v11

## Situation

High German line. Through balls into the channel. Algeria's runners kept winning the first ball in behind. Neuer started high enough to win the race.

## Decision

Come. Early. Commit. Clear or smother before the attacker sets.

## Why it worked

Starting height was already in the middle third. First two steps were the decision. Defenders knew he was coming.

## Amateur translation

Do not stand on the 18 when your back line is on the 18. Rec keepers lose this race because they start on the goal line. Train the start position first. Then train the race. Do not install a 60-yard sweep behind a rec back four.

## Do not copy

60-yard sprints as a default. Rec recovery and rec centre-backs will not support that.
