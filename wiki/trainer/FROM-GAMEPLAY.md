# From gameplay

The link between film and the body. Trainer may not prescribe a tactic.

| What the tape showed | Physical work if NOOP allows |
|---|---|
| Late off the line on breaks | First-step acceleration, 8–12m sprints, come/stay decisions. Not extra diving |
| Hugging the six when ball is central | Positioning walkthrough, no extra load |
| Collapsing set on shots | Isometric set holds, short reaction work |
| Heavy landing, high NOOP strain after | Drop volume. Mobility. No extra plyo |
| Good decisions, body looked fine | Do not invent work |

Knowledge problems go back to positional. Do not load them.
