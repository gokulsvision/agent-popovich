## Rule

- Every goal. No cap. A 5-4 game gets 9 goal notes.
- Turnovers that created or killed a chance, or that repeat a listed GAP.
- Then the quota, skipping anything already above: 2 goalie, 2 outfield, 1 set piece.
- If the tape has no corner, say so. Do not fill the slot with another dive.

Sixty turnovers stay on the part files. This page shows the ones that teach.
