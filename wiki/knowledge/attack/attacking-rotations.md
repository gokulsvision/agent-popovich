# Attacking rotations

Named movement patterns. They all obey the same two rules: one player per lane, and if the pass is not on you reset the shape.

A rotation is not "everyone run." It is a trigger, a first mover, a fill, and a reset.

**Format:** futsal. Rec 11v11 takes pass-and-move and the vacated lane, not the court geometry.

## Floor balance, always

3-1 uses three lanes at the back plus a pivot providing depth. 4-0 uses four vertical lanes. Two bodies in one lane is the error. The next action is: someone leaves.

If a diagonal run is not played, the runner drops back. A failed run that resets is fine. A failed run that stays is a crowd.

Cue: **he leaves, you fill. If it is not on, come back.**

## The eight (figure-eight)

Usually the first 3-1 rotation beginners learn. Players move toward the ball carrier to offer support, tracing an 8.

Trigger: we have it, they are set, we need a simple support pattern.
Failure: everyone moves toward the ball, the far side dies, the defence reads it because the patterns are few.
Cue: **support the ball. Do not all arrive.**

Rec translation: teach "pass and take the space you left" before you name this.

## Redondo (circular / false pivot)

A player moves from a side lane into the central lane, arriving from the defender's blind side. Used against defences that build multiple lines. Feels like a halfway point between 3-1 and 4-0. Often the stepping stone into quatro.

Trigger: they are layered. The central pocket is empty for a moment.
Failure: the runner arrives too early and blocks the fixo's passing lane.
Cue: **arrive from their blind side. Do not block the pass you came for.**

## Pivot and fixo switch

If two players can play pivot, swapping the high reference with a deeper player forces a defensive handoff. The dropping pivot arrives from the blind side and is free for a moment.

Trigger: their last man is glued to our pivot.
Failure: both go high. Nobody is behind the ball.
Cue: **one drops, one goes. Not both.**

## Diagonal run, opposite-side drop

A diagonal run. The pivot drops on the opposite side. The 3-1 shape stays clean.

Trigger: the lane in front of a deeper player is empty.
Failure: the pivot does not drop, two players occupy the same high space.
Cue: **diagonal. Opposite side fills.**

## Line cuts (3-1 into 2-2 or 4-0)

If a deeper player runs through and does not drop, the shape becomes 2-2. European sides combine 3-1 and 4-0 this way. Defenders have to change their response, which creates hesitation.

Rec translation: a rec team will not time this. The idea that travels is: a run that stays high changes the picture. Call it that, not a formation change.

## Quatro rotation families

Four types, mixed in a real attack:

1. **2-player.** Ala and fixo on the same side swap. Creates a two-player relationship between the two deep players: backdoor, diagonal, central parallel. Easiest. Easy to read. Entry point only.
2. **3-player with a fixed fixo (axis).** The professional default. Builds "3 on line" then a triangle. The 3 on line draws defenders, then the usual breaks: third-man, skip pass, backdoor against mark exchanges, ala corta (receiving in the short wide pocket), entre lineas.
3. **3-player with a fixed side.**
4. **4-player.** All four interchange.

Double run in behind is the quatro attacking option that actually hurts: two forward passing lanes, cover arrives late. If the pass is not played, restore the lanes.

Against mark-exchanging defences, the third-man run through the middle of a 3 on line is dead. Use the backdoor into the space the switch just left.

When forming the triangle after switching the ball to the far side, the supporting player must not arrive so early that they block the fixo's pass. Support slowly. Keep the lane open the whole way.

Cue: **two in one lane is the mistake. Restore it.**

## Rec translation

For this team, rotation means three things:

1. After you pass, move.
2. Fill the space the passer left, or the space the defender left when they followed.
3. If two of you are in the same pocket, one leaves.

Name the eight only after those three are visible on tape. Do not name quatro.

## Do not install

A 4-0 playbook. A weekly new rotation. Court-lane numbers quoted on an 11v11 pitch. A pattern that needs four people who trained it twice this week when six showed.
