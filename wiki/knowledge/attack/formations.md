# Formations

The four outfield players, keeper excluded. A formation is the shape you drop into without the ball, and the starting picture with it. On the attack it is a framework, not a cage. Players rotate. Obsessing about the exact shape while you have the ball kills the next pass.

**Format:** futsal 5+1. Rec 11v11 does not copy these numbers. Distances and rest defence stay on 11v11 pages.

Two principles sit under every shape, from amateur coaching literature:

- Somebody is attacking. Depth is as important as width.
- Somebody is committed to defending. The last man is a job, not a personality.

If you neglect either against a decent side, you struggle.

## 3-1 (diamond)

Three deep, one high. Fixo at the base, two alas, a pivot.

**Good at:** balance. Natural triangles. Width in the middle of the court. Clear jobs: one attacker, one last man, two who support both.

**Bad at:** both alas going forward together and leaving the fixo alone. The two wide players have to cover a lot of ground.

**Player profile:** a pivot who can hold the ball. Alas with lungs. A disciplined fixo.

**How it is countered:** man-to-man on the pivot kills the high reference. A high press on the three-at-the-back forces the long ball. If the alas do not recover, it is a 2v1 the other way.

**Rec translation:** this is the default. Teach it as a defensive shape first. Attack is fluid.

Cue: **one high, one back, two in the middle. Do not all go.**

## 4-0 (quatro)

No fixed pivot. All four play deeper and interchange. Huge space in front for runs in behind. Confuses a set defence if the movement is real.

**Good at:** playing out under pressure. Breaking a zonal defence that is waiting for a target. Creating two forward passing lanes at once.

**Bad at:** crowding your own half. Needs work rate, technique, and the four-lane rule. Easy to read if you only use the two-player swap.

**Player profile:** four universals. A pivot who can still receive with their back to goal in deeper areas is useful even here. Teams sometimes use 4-0 only while playing out, then change into 3-1 past halfway.

**How it is countered:** a compact half-court. Do not chase every interchange. Protect the run in behind first.

**Rec translation:** do not install this as a season system. A two-player swap on one side (fixo and ala exchange) is the only 4-0 fragment worth teaching first.

Cue: **nobody parked up top. The run in behind is the point.**

## 2-2 (square / box)

Two and two. No midfield. Clear attack and defence jobs.

**Good at:** simple. Direct. Two forwards against one last man is a lot to handle on a small court. Beginners understand it.

**Bad at:** transitions. Forwards stop tracking. Defenders stop joining. Possession play has no link. Communication failures are expensive.

**How it is countered:** occupy the space between the two lines. The gap in the middle is the whole problem.

Cue: **two stay, two go. If a defender joins, the other stays.**

## 2-1-1 (pyramid)

Two defenders, one midfielder, one striker. Narrow. A central link that most 5v5 shapes do not have.

**Good at:** playing out. Cover if one defender joins. Counter-attacking against a stronger side.

**Bad at:** width. Without wide players, width has to come from a defender leaving their post or a striker drifting, both of which break the shape.

Cue: **narrow on purpose. Width is a run, not a station.**

## 1-1-2

One defender, one midfielder, two forwards. Maximum pressure. Vulnerable. Needs forwards who track.

**Rec translation:** a chase-the-game shape, not a default. Same family as pulling the keeper. Do not live here.

## How to pick one

Start simple. The formation is the **defending** shape. On the attack, rotate inside it.

A rec team with mixed attendance should pick 3-1 or 2-2 and stay there for months. Switching every week is a new system fighting whoever showed up.

On 11v11 the idea that travels is not the number. It is: one high reference, one last man, do not all run at the ball.

## Rec translation

Install 3-1 as the drop-back shape. Name the last man. Name the highest player. That is the formation.

## Do not install

4-0 as a full system. A new shape every match. An 11v11 diamond copied from this page onto a pitch.
