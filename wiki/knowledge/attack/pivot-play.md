# Pivot play

The furthest player forward. In the literature: pivô, pivo, pivot. Two jobs at once: a focal point the rest of the attack plays off, and a player who can turn (pivot) around a marker.

Even without the ball the position matters. Holding high stretches the defence vertically. That is depth. Teams that all drift toward the ball play in a cramped half and every pass is contested.

**Format:** futsal. On 11v11 the idea that travels is "the highest player pins." Distances do not.

## Who plays it

Not the biggest body. Someone who can:

- Put a foot on the ball and keep it under pressure
- Anticipate the pass and the loose ball
- Control a firm pass with their back to goal
- Turn, or lay it off first time
- Finish when the chance is actually on

A false pivot is a high player who drifts wide to open the middle. Useful as a variation. If they always drift, you have no depth.

## How to receive

A half-decent last man will not let you turn for free. So:

- Check away, then come to the ball. Standing still is easy to mark.
- Body between defender and ball. Protect it.
- First thought: can I turn. If not, the bounce (wall pass) to the arriving teammate is the whole trick.
- Do not take a touch into traffic because a compilation did.

The bounce is not a failure. It is the point. The arriving ala or fixo plays forward into the space the pivot just occupied.

Cue: **hold it or give it first time. Do not take a hopeful extra touch.**

## When to spin, when to hold

Spin when the touch is heavy, the marker is square, or you already have them on your back and a half-turn is on.

Hold when the marker is set, help is arriving, or turning would lose it in a dangerous area.

Cue: **if you are not sure, lay it off.**

## What the others do

The pivot pins. The nearest player shows under. The rest rotate into the vacated lane. If both the pivot and a second player go high, there is nobody to play through.

Cue: **highest player pins. Nearest shows under.**

## Against the pivot, briefly

The last man marks the furthest player. Intercept only if you win it. If there is doubt, get close enough that they cannot turn, and wait. After the ball goes into the pivot, recover to the ball line. A second defender can leave the opposing fixo and double the pivot.

That is defending. The attacking note is: if they double you, the free man is behind them. Lay it off.

## Rec translation

On futsal: one player stays high. Receive, protect, lay off. Do not both crash the same pocket.

On 11v11: the highest player pins their last man. That is the whole translation. Do not ask a rec centre-forward to play like a futsal pivot for 90 minutes.

## Do not install

A specialist who never defends. A false-pivot rotation as a first teaching. Asking the keeper to be the pivot.
