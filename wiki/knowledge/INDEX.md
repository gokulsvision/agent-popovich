# Knowledge

How the game is actually played. This is the library, not the playbook.

## Knowledge is not doctrine

Keep the distinction or the agent starts reciting a textbook instead of coaching this team.

| | Knowledge | Doctrine |
|---|---|---|
| Answers | How futsal works everywhere | What we do |
| Example | The five families of corner defence exist | We defend corners with a hybrid |
| Changes when | Someone learns the game better | We decide differently |
| Written by | Research from public sources | A human on this team |
| Cites | Sources, studies, terminology | Our own match pages |

Knowledge widens the options. Doctrine picks one. A gap is a hole in doctrine, never a hole in knowledge.

Never coach a page from here at this team without translating it. Every page carries a **Rec translation** and a **Do not install** block for exactly that reason. An elite rotation taught to eight people who trained twice makes the team worse, not better.

## Load on demand only

Do not read this library before answering. SKILL.md's working memory is TEAM, STRENGTHS, GAPS, KEEPER, THIS-WEEK and the last five match pages.

Open one page here when a specific question needs it:

- naming what happened on tape ("that was a redondo, it broke because nobody held the far lane")
- explaining why a fix works
- choosing between real options rather than inventing one

Then close it. A coaching answer is still one item, one cue.

## Terminology

Positions, in the language the literature uses.

| Role | Also called | Job in one line |
|---|---|---|
| **Goleiro** | goalkeeper, GK | Keeps it out, starts the attack, sometimes the fifth attacker |
| **Fixo** | fijo, last man, defender, anchor | Deepest outfield player. Defensive reference and the start of build-up |
| **Ala** | winger, laterale, wide | The two flanks. Width in attack, first pressure out of it |
| **Pivô** | pivot, pivo, target | Highest player. Back to goal, pins the last defender, creates depth |
| **Universal** | universale | A player who covers every outfield role. What rotation systems need |

Numbers describe the shape of the four outfield players only, keeper excluded. A 3-1 is three deep and a pivot. A 4-0 has no fixed pivot.

Sources disagree on spelling (pivô, pivo, pivot) and on where a high press ends and a half-court press begins. Say which you mean rather than assuming the reader shares your definition.
