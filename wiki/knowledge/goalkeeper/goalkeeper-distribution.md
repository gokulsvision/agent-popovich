# Goalkeeper distribution

Simple first. Be a pass option. Do not play like City.

The laws make this a tactical weapon, not a flourish. See [[laws-that-shape-play]].

**Format:** futsal laws below. 11v11 goal kicks are on [[restarts]].

## Four seconds

In your own half you cannot have the ball, hands or feet, for more than four seconds. The referee counts. A slow restart when you are winning is legal and correct. A slow restart when you are losing is how you stay losing.

Cue: **winning, take your time. Losing, go.**

## You cannot have it back

Once you have played it in your own half, you cannot receive it again in your own half until an opponent touches it. Pass-back trap. Rec teams walk into this constantly.

Cue: **I have used my touch. Do not play it back.**

## Goal clearance

No punt. Throw or roll. Must leave within four seconds. Opponents outside the area until it is in play.

When you save it and it stays in, you may throw anywhere. When it has gone out, many rec leagues require you to play it on your side first. Check the local rule. FIFA and rec leagues disagree here.

Cue: **throw to the free player. If there is not one, go long and let us compete.**

## Hands vs feet in their half

In the opposition half there is no four-second or pass-back restriction on your feet. That is why a fly keeper who has crossed halfway can play like an outfield player. In your own half, the clock is running.

## Rec translation

Short to the free player. Long if pressed. Throw if the target is open and closer than a kick. Do not dribble into traffic. Tempo is a choice: fast when behind, slow when ahead.

## Do not install

Build-out against a press this team has not trained. Keeper dribbling as a default.
