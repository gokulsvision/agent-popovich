# Goalkeeper fundamentals

Futsal keeping is its own trade. The ball is heavier and lower-bounce. The court is small. Shots come from point-blank range. You play with your feet. You restart with your hands. You have four seconds. You cannot have it back in your own half until an opponent touches it.

An 11v11 keeper who "just stands in the futsal goal" will be late, too deep, and diving at balls they should block.

**Format:** futsal first. 11v11 distances and come-or-stay live on [[starting-position]] and [[come-off-the-line]].

## Starting position

Play the angle, not the line. You are closer to the action than an 11v11 keeper, so a yard off the line is a different decision. Stay big. Quiet feet before the shot. Hands ready.

On 11v11, height is by third of the pitch. That rule does not transfer to a court. Do not quote the penalty spot on futsal tape.

Cue: **see the ball, see the near post. Set before they hit it.**

## Stay tall

Futsal keepers are coached to stay standing as long as possible. A dive is a last resort. Once you are on the ground, the rebound is a goal. Point-blank shots are blocked, not flown at.

Cue: **make yourself big. The dive is last.**

## 1v1 and near post

Delay. Do not dive early. Near post is yours. Crossing (the star / barrier) is the 1v1 shape: lower the feet, close the shot, take away the dribble. See [[blocking-technique]].

Cue: **near post. Stay up. Force them wide.**

## Second ball

Rebounds are the sport. Where you recover after the first contact is the save. A blocked shot that sits is still live.

Cue: **get up. The next one is coming.**

## Mindset, without pep

You will concede. The court is small. Get the ball out of the net and set for the next one. Focus is a technical requirement, not a speech. A keeper who drops after a goal is beaten twice.

## Rec translation

On futsal: angles, stay tall, block close shots, throw quickly or slowly on purpose.

On 11v11: starting height and come-or-stay are the whole game. Do not import court blocking as a default on a pitch.

## Do not install

An 11v11 sweep as a futsal default. Extra diving as the training plan. Pep.
