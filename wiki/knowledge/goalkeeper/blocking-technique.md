# Blocking technique

The futsal-specific save. Close-range shots, penalties, double penalties, 1v1s. Called crossing, the star, the barrier, the K. You make a wide shape, drop your feet, and close the shot instead of flying.

**Format:** futsal. On 11v11, set and make yourself big still matter. The full star on a penalty-spot shot from 18 yards is usually the wrong tool.

## When to use it

Shot is close. Attacker is set. You cannot win a race to smother. Penalty or 10m accumulated-foul kick.

You are trying to take away both the shot and the dribble past you. A conventional dive gives them the dribble and the rebound.

## How

- Lower the feet, do not collapse the whole body
- Make a wide barrier
- Close the near side
- Stay big through the contact
- Then get up for the rebound

A slightly lifted near-side shot that a wall cannot reach is the finishing answer to a bad block. Aiming high travels through hip height, which is harder to block than a ground ball.

Cue: **big. Near post. Do not dive.**

## When not

Long-range. A ball you can catch. A through ball you should come for. Diving at a ball you could have stayed tall on is the amateur error.

On 11v11, collapsing the set because the shot "looks hard" is the same family of mistake. Isometric set holds if the tape shows it and recovery allows. Not extra plyo.

Cue: **if you have time to catch it, catch it.**

## Rec translation

Stay standing. Block what is close. Dive last. Train the shape walking-pace first on a yellow recovery day.

## Do not install

Blocking as the only save. Extra diving volume because a compilation of stars looked good.
