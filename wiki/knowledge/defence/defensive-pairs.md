# Defensive pairs

Challenge and cover. The 1st defender goes. The 2nd covers. The 3rd talks. Without the pair, a press is just a runner.

**Format:** futsal roles. The idea travels: nearest presses, next covers, deepest organises.

## 1st defender

Press the ball carrier when you can actually arrive. Close to about an arm's length is the trigger to hunt, not just jockey. Take away the lane in behind and the pass into the pivot.

If you cannot arrive, do not dive in. Delay. Show them one way.

Cue: **can I get there. If yes, go. If no, delay.**

## 2nd defender

Cover. Screen the skip pass. Protect the central pocket if the 1st is not pressing. Hunt the horizontal if the 1st is.

Wrong: jumping to the ball and leaving the 1st with nobody behind them.

Cue: **he goes, you cover.**

## 3rd defender

Float. See the whole picture. Call **stay** or **go with him**. When the high press is beaten, this is the person who organises the three-player rotation back.

On half-court the last man is often doing this job plus marking the furthest attacker.

Cue: **talk. If you are quiet, we are guessing.**

## Double team

If the 2nd is already close, two can trap the ball. Only if the 1st has taken away the lane in behind and the pass to the pivot. Then leaving your mark is legal.

After the ball goes into the pivot, recover to the ball line. The 4th (marking their fixo) can leave and double the pivot.

Cue: **two on the ball only if the pass in behind is dead.**

## Rec translation

Nearest man. Next man covers. Last man talks. Three jobs. If audio is dead on tape, you cannot score the talk. Mark `call: unknown`.

## Do not install

A numbering system nobody will use on a Tuesday. Double-teaming as a default, which opens the far man.
