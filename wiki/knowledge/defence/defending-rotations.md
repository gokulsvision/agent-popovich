# Defending rotations

How a defence survives movement. The attack is trying to make you take the same man twice and leave one free. That is the classic breakdown.

**Format:** futsal. On 11v11 stay at unit level ("the highest player") until TEAM.md has formation and colours.

## Follow vs switch

Man-to-man default: follow the runner. Do not switch.

Exception: a block or curtain. Fighting through the screen is what the offence wants. Switch marks.

Cue: **runner goes, you go. Screen, you switch.**

## Slide

Two defenders exchange roles. Used when the ball is switched heavily and the press is built on multiple lines. A backward or negative ala-to-ala pass is usually a slide. A flat or advancing skip is usually a rotation.

Cue: **ball switched back. We swap.**

## Rotate

Three players exchange on a beaten high press. Four players on a beaten half-court. Used when they have advanced a long way.

Cue: **we are beaten. Rotate, do not chase in a straight line.**

## Retreat

The structure is gone. Drop as a block. Protect the goal. Win it later.

Cue: **if cover cannot arrive, drop.**

## Body and vision

See mark and ball. Far post is a useful reference on set pieces because it lets you keep both in view. If you cannot, check over the shoulder.

Distance: far enough that you are not beaten in behind. Close enough that they cannot turn for free when they receive.

Cue: **goal side. See both.**

## Against specific pictures

**Pivot with back to goal.** Get close enough they cannot turn. Intercept only if you win it. After the pass in, recover to the ball line. A second body can double.

**Parallel / run in behind.** Protect that lane first. A missed interception as last man is a 1v1 against the keeper.

**Screen.** Switch.

**Backdoor.** The switch itself created the hole. If you switch, the original marker has to recover the space they left, or the 3rd has to cover it.

**2v1 / 3v2 counter.** Delay. Do not dive. Show them the outside. Keeper comes or stays as a separate decision.

## Rec translation

Know who is yours. If you do not, you are marking space you were not given. That is a gap. Switch on a block. Drop if beaten. Do not invent a rotation name for a scramble.

## Do not install

A switching syllabus for a team that does not yet drop as a block. Following runners into the box on a corner and leaving the penalty spot empty.
