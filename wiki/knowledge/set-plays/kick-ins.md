# Kick-ins

Because you cannot score directly from an attacking-half kick-in, the standard solution is **chon-don**: the kicker taps it, a shooter hits it first time.

Simple, and often unstoppable even when the defence knows it is coming. You do not need a library of kick-in routines. Master this and you score.

**Format:** futsal. 11v11 throw-ins are not this page. See [[restarts]].

## The wall, defending

Stand 5m from the ball. Two in the wall: near side and far side. If the angle is dead, one may be enough. Once the ball is in play, the far-side wall attacks the ball. Many teams build the wall from the near side first and keep that player fixed (stone).

The wall cannot fully take away the pass to the segundo. Often the wall takes the near shot and the keeper takes the far side and the segundo.

If the shooter is close, the wall stays tight so it does not split. If the shooter has a long run-up, the far-side wall starts on the line to the far post and connects at the moment of the shot. Keeper organises from behind.

Cue: **near side stone. Far side attacks the ball.**

## Chon-don, attacking

As soon as the kicker taps, they run around behind the shooter. That covers the rebound and forces the wall to split.

Finishing triangle at the shot: shooter, segundo, rebound, plus a balance player.

**Pattern 1: tap and shoot.** Aim near side, shortest route. A slightly lifted near-side shot the keeper cannot reach with a leg, or the top corner. A rising ball through hip height is harder for a wall to block than a ground ball.

**Pattern 2: segundo or pivot.** When the wall splits, the lane to the segundo opens. Use it.

Other attacking-half patterns exist (kicker change, diagonal runner, 1-3). Rec teams do not need them yet.

Cue: **tap, hit it first time. If the wall splits, segundo.**

## Own-half kick-ins

Different job. Keep it. Sometimes the keeper joins as a fifth. That is a designed 5v4, not panic. Rec translation: play simple, do not lose it on our line.

## Rec translation

One attacking routine: tap and shoot, near side. One defending picture: two in the wall, far side steps. Balance player stays. That is the week.

## Do not install

Five kick-in choreographies. Chon-don on an 11v11 throw-in.
