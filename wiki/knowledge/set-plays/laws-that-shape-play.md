# Laws that shape play

Only the rules that change a decision. Not a full law book. FIFA, US Youth Futsal, and rec leagues disagree in places. If TEAM.md does not say which laws you play, ask, then write it down.

**Format:** futsal. 11v11 has offside, throw-ins, and no four-second count. Do not quote these on a pitch.

## The court and the ball

Smaller court. Low-bounce size 4 (or size 3 for younger). No walls in true futsal: the line is out. 5-a-side with boards is a different game. Name which you are watching on the match page.

Tactical consequence: every turnover is immediately dangerous. There is no hidden space.

## No offside

You may hold a player high. Depth is legal.

Consequence: the pivot is a real position. Rest defence matters more, because a loss is a free run.

## Kick-in, not throw-in

Ball on the line. Four seconds. Indirect: a kick-in that goes straight in is not a goal. You cannot score directly.

Consequence: kick-ins are set plays. See [[kick-ins]]. Rec teams treat them like throw-ins and waste them.

## Four seconds on restarts

Kick-in, corner, goal clearance, and (in FIFA) the keeper in their own half. Referee often counts visibly.

Consequence: slow when winning, fast when losing. A wasted four seconds is a turnover.

## Goal clearance

Throw or roll. No punt. Opponents outside the area.

Consequence: the keeper starts every attack with the hands. Distribution is a job, not a bonus.

## Pass-back in your own half

After the keeper plays it, they cannot have it back in their own half until an opponent touches it. Hands or feet.

Consequence: you cannot use the keeper as a safe bounce once they have touched it. Rec teams concede cheap free kicks here.

## Unlimited flying subs

Through the substitution zone in front of your bench. Enter after the other player leaves. Teams switch benches at half so subs happen in the defensive half.

Consequence: intensity can stay high. Rec consequence: if six showed, this does not help you.

## Accumulated fouls

Direct-free-kick fouls add up each half. From the sixth, the other team gets a direct free kick from the spot or from the 10m mark (second penalty mark), depending on where the foul was. The count resets at half.

Consequence: late in a half, fouling is a tactic with a price. Defending a fly keeper by chopping is how you give them a 10m with an empty net. Rec referees are inconsistent. Still teach the idea.

## No slide tackle

In most futsal, sliding in is a foul (local leagues vary).

Consequence: defending is on your feet. Delay, jockey, block with the body.

## Timeout

One per half, when you have the ball and it is out.

Consequence: use it when chasing, or when the picture is chaos. Do not spend it at 2-0 in the first half.

## Rec translation

Remember four: kick-in is a set play, four seconds, no pass-back to the keeper, sixth foul is a 10m. Everything else is local. Write the local exceptions in TEAM.md.

## Do not install

A law lecture as a session. FIFA detail that this rec league does not use.
