# Corners (futsal)

The standard attacking corner is a triangle. The five ways to defend it are zone Y, zone box, man-to-man, mixed, and late-pick-up man-to-man. Our 11v11 corner jobs live on [[attacking-corners]] and [[defending-corners]]. Do not mix the two geometries.

**Format:** futsal.

## Attacking: triangle setup

- Front two on the penalty-area line
- Deeper player on the second penalty mark

Target zones, most dangerous first: inside, front side, outside. Decent teams will not leave the inside open, so you attack in reverse order: outside, then front, then inside.

**Outside:** front player circles, feigns a shot, blocks, lets it run. Deeper player hits a mid-range shot, ideally first time. Far player attacks the second post. Taker drops as rest defence. If the shooter is on the opposite foot, volley instead. On a volley the kicker becomes the second-post runner.

**Front side:** if they start dropping toward the deeper option, the front player shoots. A fake block (show the screen, let them slip around it, use the free moment) is the variation.

Who stands where is the whole set play. Set up fast. Qualities to the zone.

Cue: **triangle. Outside first. Someone stays.**

Sporting's near-side routine is a known elite pattern. Rec teams do not need it. Near post occupied is the translation.

## Defending: five families

1. **Y-zone (2-1-1).** Most common. Defend a zone or a passing lane. People are bad at covering both front and back at once. Take one side. Do not stick a foot at a ball played behind you: that is how own goals happen. Leave balls behind you to the player behind.
2. **Box-zone (2-2).** Easier on the deepest ball, thinner in the middle.
3. **All man-to-man.** Near-side player has the kicker. Switch on a block. Stay far-post side so you can see mark and ball.
4. **Late man-to-man.** Pick up just before the run.
5. **Mixed.** Triangle-and-one.

Cue: **you have a job before the ball is kicked. If you do not know it, you are the gap.**

## Rec translation

Futsal: one attacking triangle, outside shot first, one rest-defence. Defend in a Y if you can remember it, otherwise nearest man plus the keeper owns the near post.

11v11: ignore this page. Use the amateur inswinger and the hybrid we already picked.

## Do not install

Five defending families in one session. Arsenal blockers. Quoting the second penalty mark on an 11v11 pitch.
